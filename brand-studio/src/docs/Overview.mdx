## The workflow

One project document moves through six screens. Every step is a lead for EventPipe, and every brand choice re-skins all the pages at once.

<ol className="ov-flow">
  <li><strong>Homepage</strong>Paste your event website, or open one of 20 industry examples.</li>
  <li><strong>Event intake</strong>Name, type, where and when, an optional logo, and contact. No account needed.</li>
  <li><strong>Brand kit</strong>Scan the website for logo, colors and fonts, check contrast, and pick photos.</li>
  <li><strong>Concepts</strong>Six centered banner concepts across three directions. Pick one, compare, make variations.</li>
  <li><strong>Studio</strong>Edit content, pages and ads beside a live preview in a real browser. Export the assets as a ZIP.</li>
  <li><strong>Publish</strong>Name, URL, review and checklist, then a shareable demo with an honest demo banner.</li>
</ol>

**Walk it with no typing.**

1. **Start.** Tap the website field (it fills with an example), then **Brand my page**. Or open an industry example to jump straight into the Studio.
2. **Event intake.** Tap any field to fill it with that example's details, then Continue through event, where and when, brand, and contact.
3. **Brand kit.** Tap **Scan my website**, then **Apply selected**. The same brand (logo, colors, fonts, photos) carries through every later step.
4. **Concepts.** Generate six concepts and select one.
5. **Studio.** Switch pages, devices and panels. **Export** previews and downloads the banners, logo, ads and page mockups as a ZIP. **Copy link** shares the exact view for feedback.
6. **Publish.** Confirm the name and URL, review, accept the demo disclaimer, and open the live demo.

**Demo assist.** On the form steps, **Fill on/off** toggles tap-to-fill and **Skip** jumps ahead. Every screen's state is in the URL, so any view can be shared as a link.

## How it's built

- **The Presto design system is reused, not rebuilt.** The real `LandingPage`, `HotelListPage`, `HotelDetailPage`, `BookingWidget` and other components render the previews. A **brand theme engine** maps each Brand Kit onto Presto's semantic tokens, so they re-skin without forks. See **[Brand Theme Engine](?path=/story/brand-studio-foundations--brand-theme-engine)**.
- **Branding is separate from page content.** One Brand Kit change updates the landing, browse and details pages at once.
- **One banner layout.** Every hero follows the Presto standard: logo, event name and dates centered over a minimal background. See **[Brand Hero](?path=/story/brand-studio-creative-concepts-brand-hero--all-layouts)**.
- **The AI makes artwork only.** Logos, headlines and buttons stay as editable, accessible text on top. Example logos, banners and marketing imagery were generated with **Higgsfield** (Ideogram, Recraft, Marketing Studio Flare). See **[Higgsfield](?path=/docs/brand-studio-higgsfield-setup-guide--docs)**.
- **Providers are swappable.** A single contract covers the mock, Higgsfield, Recraft and Ideogram. Real providers run through a secure EventPipe backend, so no API keys reach the browser.
- **State is serializable.** One versioned project document, ready for Supabase.

Details are in **[Architecture & Audit](?path=/docs/brand-studio-architecture-audit--docs)**.
