# Presto Brand Studio

Event organizers design and preview a **branded hotel booking experience** for their event in minutes. They enter event details and branding, pick an AI-assisted creative concept, and customize a realistic Presto booking site. When it looks right, they publish a shareable demo. Every step is a lead for EventPipe.

**How to walk it, with no typing.**

1. **Start.** Tap **Create Your Booking Page**, or **See a sample** to jump straight into the editor with a finished project.
2. **Event intake.** Tap a field and it fills with sample data (the Heartland Invitational Soccer Cup). Pick a category card, then Continue through the steps: event, where and when, brand, contact.
3. **Brand discovery.** Tap **Scan my website**. The mock scanner finds the sample logo, colors and fonts. Apply them, or tweak colors, fonts and the hero photo.
4. **Concepts.** Tap **Generate six concepts** to get six concepts across three directions. Select one; you can also favorite, compare, regenerate or make variations.
5. **Studio editor.** Switch pages (landing, browse, details), devices, and the Brand, Content, Pages, Ads and Concept panels. The live preview is the real Presto booking site.
6. **Publish.** Confirm the name and URL, mark the desktop and mobile previews reviewed, accept the demo disclaimer, and sign up (auto-filled). Then open your live demo.

**Demo assist** is in the top bar on the form steps. **Fill on/off** controls tap-to-fill; **Skip** jumps ahead with sample data.

**What's simulated.** The AI generator is a free mock, and Higgsfield, Recraft and Ideogram connect later through a secure backend. Sign-in is mocked and drafts save to this browser. The hotels are Presto prototype mock data. Published demos are labelled as demos, with no live rates, bookings or payments.

## How it's built

- **The Presto design system is reused, not rebuilt.** The real `LandingPage`, `HotelListPage`, `HotelDetailPage`, `BookingWidget` and other components render the previews. A **brand theme engine** maps each Brand Kit onto Presto's semantic tokens, so they re-skin without forks. See **[Brand Theme Engine](?path=/story/brand-studio-foundations--brand-theme-engine)**.
- **Branding is separate from page content.** One Brand Kit change updates the landing, browse and details pages at once.
- **The AI makes artwork only.** Logos, headlines and buttons stay as editable, accessible text on top. This is the **[Brand Hero](?path=/story/brand-studio-creative-concepts-brand-hero--all-layouts)**.
- **Providers are swappable.** A single contract covers the mock, Higgsfield, Recraft and Ideogram. Real providers run through a secure EventPipe backend, so no API keys reach the browser.
- **State is serializable.** One versioned project document, ready for Supabase.

Details are in **[Architecture & Audit](?path=/docs/brand-studio-architecture-audit--docs)**.
